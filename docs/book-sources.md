# Book data and cover sources

Metadata and plot outlines were checked against the catalogue records below. Descriptions are short paraphrases prepared for this prototype, not copied publisher descriptions. Categories are the project's browsing categories; some works fit more than one genre.

Publication years refer to original book publication, rather than the upload dates or dates of the digitised editions. The Hound of the Baskervilles was serialised in 1901-1902; its book year is 1902. Around the World in Eighty Days was serialised in 1872; its book year is 1873. Journey to the Centre of the Earth uses the original French publication year, 1864.

Cover JPEGs are saved from the individual Project Gutenberg cover endpoints. These may be historical edition covers or Gutenberg-generated covers; they are not claimed to be original first-edition artwork. This pack contains no ebook texts.
